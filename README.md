RecurSaathi – Your Revenue Recovery Partner

RecurSaathi is an intelligent revenue recovery platform designed to help Indian small businesses recover lost revenue, manage overdue invoices, track failed payments and reconnect with inactive customers.

With powerful analytics and automated email and WhatsApp reminders, RecurSaathi helps businesses identify revenue leaks, improve payment collections and build stronger customer relationships.

Key Features:

- Revenue Recovery Radar
- Automated overdue invoice tracking
- Failed card and UPI payment monitoring
- Dormant customer identification
- Smart email and WhatsApp automation
- Customer and invoice management
- Direct business payment collection
- Real-time revenue analytics
- Recovery performance reports

Our Mission: Help every small business reduce revenue leakage, recover more money and grow smarter with automation.

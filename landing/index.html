Frequently asked questions

Do you hold my customers' money?

No. Customer invoices pay your own Razorpay account or your own UPI ID. RecurSaathi automates, tracks and reports. Money never flows through us.

Do I need Razorpay to get started?

No. Manual UPI mode works from day one: generate a payment page and QR for your UPI ID, and mark the invoice paid when the money lands. Connect Razorpay whenever you're ready for automatic retries and webhook detection.

What happens before I connect email and WhatsApp?

Automations are built and queued, and every message is honestly marked "no channel" instead of pretending to send. Connect your SMTP or WhatsApp Business API keys and the queue starts flowing. Nothing is simulated.

Are retries RBI-compliant?

Yes. UPI Autopay and card retry windows follow RBI rules, including the 24-hour, 72-hour and day-7 windows. We never blind-retry hard declines like invalid cards.

Is my data safe?

We store gateway tokens, never card numbers or PANs. Passwords are salted and hashed. Every webhook is signature-verified, and every automation action lands in an audit trail.

Can customers opt out of reminders?

Yes. Every message carries an opt-out link, and opted-out customers are excluded from all future reminders automatically.

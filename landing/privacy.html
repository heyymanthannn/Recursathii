Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What we collect

Account details (name, business name, email, phone), your UPI ID if you add one, and the customer and invoice records you enter. If you connect Razorpay, your API keys are stored encrypted and never displayed again in full.

Customer data

You control the customer data you upload. We process it only to provide the service: sending reminders you configure, showing payment pages and keeping your records. We do not sell data or use it to train third-party AI models.

Payments

Customer invoice payments settle directly into your own bank account via your own UPI ID or your own Razorpay account. RecurSaathi never holds customer money. Subscription payments to RecurSaathi are separate.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sensitive credentials are encrypted at rest. Gateway webhooks are verified by signature. Access is role-based, and administrative actions are logged.

Your rights

Write to recursathi@gmail.com to access, correct or delete your data. Customer opt-outs from WhatsApp are honored automatically.

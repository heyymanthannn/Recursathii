Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

Account details (name, business name, email, phone), your UPI ID and payee name if you add one, and the customer, invoice and payment records you enter or import. If you connect Razorpay or a WhatsApp Business number, the credentials needed to run that connection (API keys, phone number ID, app secret) are stored encrypted and never shown again in full.

Customer data

You control the customer data you add. We process it only to provide the service: sending the reminders you configure, showing payment pages and links, keeping your records, and answering customer requests that reach your connected channels. We do not sell customer data and we do not use it to train third-party AI models.

Messages on your behalf

Reminders are sent through channels your business connects (email, WhatsApp). We honor customer opt-outs automatically: a customer who replies STOP or opts out through the portal stops receiving reminders. Message logs (delivery status, timestamps) are kept so you can see what was sent.

Payments

Customer invoice payments settle directly into your own bank account via your own UPI ID or your own Razorpay account. RecurSaathi never receives, holds or transfers customer money. Subscription payments to RecurSaathi are separate and processed by our payment provider.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sensitive credentials are encrypted at rest. Gateway and platform webhooks are verified by signature before processing. Access is role-based, and administrative actions are logged. Daily encrypted backups are kept off the application server.

Data retention and deletion

Your records are kept while your account is active. On written request we delete your account data, including customer records and message logs, within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep a record.

Your rights

Write to recursathi@gmail.com to access, correct or delete your data. Customer opt-outs from WhatsApp are honored automatically and independently of your account settings.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and note the date above.

The service

RecurSaathi is a communication-first revenue recovery tool for small businesses in India: it tracks recurring invoices, sends reminder sequences over email and WhatsApp on your behalf, tracks delivery, and collects customer requests (more time, payment claims, disputes) into an inbox you review.

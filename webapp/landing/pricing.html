Pricing questions

How much does RecurSaathi cost?

Starter is ₹999 per month, Growth is ₹4,999 per month and Enterprise is ₹9,999 per month. Annual billing is ₹9,990, ₹49,990 and ₹99,990 a year respectively. Prices exclude applicable taxes.

Can I pay monthly or annually?

Yes. Every plan is available monthly or annually. Starter and Growth annual plans include 2 months free.

Does RecurSaathi handle my customers' payments?

No. Your money never passes through RecurSaathi. Recovery runs on your own Razorpay account, your own WhatsApp Business number and your own SMTP login.

Which plan includes customer reactivation?

Dormant customer reactivation is part of Growth, and Enterprise includes everything in Growth. See how customer reactivation works.

What is the early-adopter Enterprise offer?

The first 10 eligible businesses pay ₹79,990 for their first 12 months of Enterprise instead of the regular ₹99,990 annual price, one offer per business. Availability is confirmed live at checkout and the offer is subject to our terms. After the first year, renewal runs at the then-current regular annual price, with advance notice and your explicit action.

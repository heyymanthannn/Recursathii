What it does

Every failed payment gets a second chance, on its own.

A customer’s renewal fails. Most businesses find out when they reconcile the month — and by then the customer is halfway gone. RecurSaathi catches it the moment it happens: retries inside RBI’s allowed windows, a WhatsApp or email nudge the same day with a one-tap UPI link, and a dashboard that shows exactly what was recovered.

The recovery assistant is rule-based automation on your own records: decline codes decide retry timing, payment behaviour decides follow-up cadence. No black boxes.

How it runs today: retries and payment links work through your own Razorpay account once it’s connected; WhatsApp nudges deliver from your own WhatsApp Business number once that’s connected; email nudges use your own SMTP login, also one connect away. Nothing pretends to be delivered before then: until a channel is connected, messages stay queued inside RecurSaathi and show as no channel. Customer money settles straight to you — it never passes through RecurSaathi.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What exactly does RecurSaathi do?

It automates payment follow-ups over your own billing records: failed card and UPI AutoPay payments get retried inside RBI’s allowed windows, overdue invoices get chased, and dormant repeat customers get flagged for win-back. The intelligence is rule-based logic on your own data — no third-party AI services.

Does customer money ever pass through RecurSaathi?

No. Payment links go to your own Razorpay account (or your own UPI ID), so money settles directly to you. RecurSaathi only reads the payment status so it knows when to stop following up.

How do retries stay within RBI rules?

Retries run through your connected Razorpay account, scheduled inside the retry windows the RBI permits for UPI AutoPay. Decline codes are read before anything is retried, so a hard decline isn’t retried blindly.

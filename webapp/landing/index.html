What it does

Four jobs, all live today.

Nothing on this page is a promise or a waitlist item. Create your account and it is already in your dashboard.

Retry

RBI-timed retries, routed by decline code, that don't quit after one attempt.

Razorpay Autopay retries

Autopay retries inside the RBI windows (24 hours, 72 hours, day 7), routed by decline code, never blind-retried on invalid cards. Signature-verified webhooks mark every result.

Nudge

Follow-ups your customer actually answers, on the channels they use.

Daily AI agent calls

Every overdue invoice gets one polite call a day, with your payment link and a promise-to-pay note. Editable scripts; calls queue in your outbox until a voice provider is connected.

Email & WhatsApp automations

Due reminders, overdue follow-ups, failed-payment nudges and payment confirmations. Editable templates, per-workflow toggles, customer opt-out.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do you hold my customers' money?+

No. Customer invoices pay your own Razorpay account or your own UPI ID. RecurSaathi automates, tracks and reports. Money never flows through us.

Do I need Razorpay to get started?+

No. Manual UPI mode works from day one: generate a payment page and QR for your UPI ID, and mark the invoice paid when the money lands. Connect Razorpay whenever you outgrow it.

What happens before I connect email and WhatsApp?+

Automations are built and queued, and every message is honestly marked "no channel" instead of pretending to send. Connect your SMTP or WhatsApp Business API key and they start going out for real.

Are retries RBI-compliant?+

Yes. UPI Autopay and card retry windows follow RBI rules, including the 24-hour, 72-hour and day-7 windows. We never blind-retry hard declines like invalid cards.

Is my data safe?+

We store gateway tokens, never card numbers or PANs. Passwords are salted and hashed. Every webhook is signature-verified, and every automation action lands in an audit trail.

Can customers opt out of reminders?+

Yes. Every message carries an opt-out link, and opted-out customers are excluded from all future reminders automatically.

What is RecurSaathi?+

RecurSaathi is a revenue-recovery tool for Indian subscription businesses. It monitors failed card and UPI payments, retries inside RBI’s allowed windows, follows up with WhatsApp and email nudges plus one-tap UPI payment links, and chases overdue invoices and dormant customers. Plans start at ₹999 a month with a 14-day free trial, no card needed. For customers outside India, plans start at $12 a month.

How do I recover failed UPI AutoPay payments in India?+

Read the failure reason from your gateway response, retry soft declines like insufficient balance inside the allowed RBI windows (24 hours, 72 hours and day 7), and send the customer a same-day WhatsApp nudge with a one-tap UPI payment link. RecurSaathi automates this ladder, and the sequence works with any gateway.

Does RecurSaathi work outside India?+

RecurSaathi is built around Indian payment rails: automated retries follow RBI windows and collection runs on UPI and Razorpay AutoPay. Businesses anywhere can use its reminders, invoices and recovery tracking, and global customers can pay in USD, but payment collection itself is India-first today.

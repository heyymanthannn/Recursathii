Product

Every overdue payment gets followed up, on its own.

A customer's payment fails or an invoice slips past its due date. Most businesses find out when they reconcile the month, and by then the customer is halfway gone. RecurSaathi catches it the moment it happens: retries inside RBI's allowed windows, a WhatsApp or email reminder the same day with a one-tap UPI link, and a dashboard that shows exactly what was recovered.

The recovery assistant is rule-based automation on your own records: decline codes decide retry timing, payment behaviour decides follow-up cadence. No black boxes.

How it runs: retries and payment links work through your own Razorpay account once it's connected; WhatsApp reminders deliver from your own WhatsApp Business number once that's connected; email reminders use your own SMTP login, also one connect away. Until a channel is connected, messages stay queued inside RecurSaathi and show as no channel. Customer money settles straight to you; it never passes through RecurSaathi.

How billing works

You get paid directly

RecurSaathi is a Revenue Recovery Communication OS, not a payment gateway. Customers pay your business with your own UPI ID, QR code or payment link, and the money settles directly to you.

We manage the tracking, the reminders and the follow-ups. Every payment confirmation is verified before an invoice is marked paid.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What exactly does RecurSaathi do?

It runs your overdue-payment follow-ups over your own billing records: failed card and UPI AutoPay payments get retried inside RBI's allowed windows, overdue invoices get chased, and dormant repeat customers get flagged for win-back. The intelligence is rule-based logic on your own data.

Does customer money ever pass through RecurSaathi?

No. Payment instructions carry your own UPI ID or your own Razorpay account, so money settles directly to you. RecurSaathi only reads the payment status so it knows when to stop following up.

How do retries stay within RBI rules?

Retries run through your connected Razorpay account, scheduled inside the retry windows the RBI permits for UPI AutoPay. Decline codes are read before anything is retried, so a hard decline isn't retried blindly.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?

WhatsApp reminders use the official Meta WhatsApp Business Platform through your own business account and number. Meta charges per conversation; those charges are yours, billed by Meta, and RecurSaathi adds nothing on top. You can start with email reminders only.

How do I get started?

Sign up, verify your email, and complete the guided onboarding: business profile, payment instructions and reminder preferences. Your 14-day trial starts right away, and automation stays off until you switch it on.

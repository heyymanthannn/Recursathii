Coaching & edtech

Fee recovery for coaching institutes and tuition centres

Batch fees, instalment plans and monthly tuition: the money is predictable until a UPI mandate fails and nobody notices for weeks.

Coaching institutes bill on instalments and batch renewals, parents pay from personal UPI accounts, and every failed debit quietly becomes a fee follow-up conversation at the reception desk.

Why payments fail here

  • Instalment schedules differ by batch and admission date, so a single reminder schedule does not fit anyone.
  • Parents pause or change mandates after fee hikes or board-exam season without telling you.
  • Partial payments and 'will pay next week' promises live in WhatsApp, not in any system.
  • A missed instalment is often a signal, not a dispute; catching it in week one keeps the student enrolled.

What RecurSaathi does about it

RecurSaathi tracks each student's fee schedule, retries failed debits within RBI's rules, sends parents a clear payment link, records verified manual payments, and shows you exactly which batches and students are slipping.

Questions people ask

We use WhatsApp groups for everything. Why a system?

WhatsApp works until you have 200 students and no record of who was asked what. RecurSaathi keeps the schedule, the history and the proof; your WhatsApp stays for teaching.

Can we track part-payments and promises?

Yes. Record what the parent committed and when; RecurSaathi follows the schedule and stops reminders when the verified payment lands.

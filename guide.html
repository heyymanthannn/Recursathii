How to recover failed subscription payments in India

Why card and UPI payments fail, how the RBI's retry windows work, and a practical dunning playbook you can run this week — with or without a payment gateway integration.

Why subscription payments fail

Most failed renewals are not fraud or non-payment. They are involuntary churn: a card expired, the UPI mandate hit its daily limit, the account was temporarily low on balance, or the bank flagged an unusual charge. The money was never refused; the payment just never went through. Every one of those failures is recoverable if you ask again, politely, at the right time.

The RBI retry windows are your friend

For recurring card mandates and UPI Autopay, the RBI defines when you may re-attempt a failed debit: roughly 24 hours, 72 hours and on day 7 after the first failure, with debit amounts above thresholds needing an additional approval step. Retrying before the window opens is not just ineffective, it can get your mandate degraded by the issuing bank.

So the biggest recovery lever in India is not "retry harder", it is retry on schedule: one attempt in each RBI window, and never blind-retrying hard declines.

What a good dunning sequence looks like

  • Day 0, payment fails: a short, human message the same day. "Your renewal didn't go through — tap here to fix it." No blame, no fine print.
  • Retry in the RBI windows: 24 hours, 72 hours, day 7, exactly on time.
  • Between retries: WhatsApp or email nudges with a one-tap payment link. Two reminders per failed attempt is plenty; more trains customers to ignore you.
  • Give an escape hatch: an easy way to pay manually (a UPI link or QR) and an easy way to opt out of reminders. Customers who feel trapped stop answering entirely.
  • Escalate gently: if day 7 fails, move the account to a slower cadence or pause it — and tell the customer what happens next.

Manual UPI is a legitimate backup

Many small Indian businesses start without an integrated gateway. That is fine: a generated payment page with a UPI QR, sent with the invoice, recovers money while you wait for gateway paperwork. The discipline that matters is marking invoices paid the moment the money lands and keeping the reminder cadence honest.

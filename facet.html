Real-time glass, in five terms.

Iridescence — thin-film interference that shifts hue with the viewing angle. Tip a shard and a green-violet sheen travels across its face.

Index of refraction — how strongly light bends on entering the glass. A higher IOR sharpens the bright rims where the slab meets the dark.

Dispersion — the index varies with wavelength, so edges split light into faint spectral fringes along every bevel.

Transmission — the fraction of light that passes through. The charcoal faces transmit little yet keep their specular highlights.

Roughness — microsurface scattering. Low roughness keeps reflections knife-sharp; higher values blur the studio lights across each piece.
